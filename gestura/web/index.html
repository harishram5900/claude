Lip reading

Reading speech from lips alone, with no sound, takes a large model (Auto-AVSR, about 250 million parameters) that needs a GPU server; it can't run inside a browser yet. Gestura measures mouth movement in every video to mark when someone is speaking, and can send the mouth region to a lip-reading server you connect.

How Gestura works

Every model runs in your browser. We tested each one on people it had never seen before putting it in front of you. Here is exactly how well each part works.

From camera to voice

  1. See the hand. Google MediaPipe finds 21 points on each hand (and, for signs, the body and face) 30 times a second.
  2. Read the letter. A small neural network we trained on 8,599 real photos of the ASL alphabet turns the 21 points into a letter. Optional calibration learns your own hand in a minute.
  3. Read the sign. For whole words, the first-place model from Google's Isolated Sign Language Recognition competition reads the motion of both hands, arms and face across the whole sign.
  4. Fix the spelling. Fingerspelled words are checked against the 10,000 most common English words.
  5. Speak. Your device's own text-to-speech voice reads the transcript aloud.

Limits we want you to know

  • ASL is a full language with its own grammar. Gestura recognises fingerspelling and 250 individual signs; it does not translate full ASL sentences yet.
  • J and Z involve motion; Gestura reads their final handshape.
  • Good light, a plain background and your whole hand in frame make a big difference.
  • Lip reading without audio needs a GPU server (see the Video page).

Data and models

  • Hand, body and face tracking: Google MediaPipe Tasks (Apache 2.0).
  • Signs: 1st-place solution, Google – Isolated Sign Language Recognition (Kaggle), MIT licence; vocabulary of 250 signs from the competition.
  • Word accuracy test and teaching animations: ASL Citizen (Desai et al., 2023), real community signers; used for non-commercial education.
  • Alphabet training: ASL alphabet photos (Hugging Face, Marxulia v03); outside test: Keithsel/asl_signs.
  • Speech: OpenAI Whisper base.en via Transformers.js.
  • Word list: Google 10,000 most common English words.
